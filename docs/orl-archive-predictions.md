# OverRustle archive (to Apr 30, 2020): predictions

Written Nov 2, 2026, 8:50 PM, after listing the archive's file names and sizes and before opening any archive.
Companion to docs/dgg-chat-glossary-predictions.md (the 2026 DGG chat study).

## Data
Twelve channel archives from the OverRustle logs torrent, picked by Joseph as DGG and adjacent communities:
Destinygg (destiny.gg chat), Destiny (Twitch chat), Hutch, H3h3productions, Denims, Asmongold, Moistcr1tikal,
Ludwig, Melina, Mindwavestv, Badbunny, Nickjfuentes. Coverage varies by channel; OverRustle logged until spring 2020.

## Rules
- Usernames are hashed at ingest (HMAC with the local laptop salt). No nick is printed or committed.
- Cross-channel overlap is reported only as aggregate counts and shares of hashed accounts. No individual is
  followed between channels.
- Bot accounts (chat bots, logger bots) are flagged by message pattern and volume and dropped before counting.
- Emotes: for Destinygg, the destiny.gg emote list (today's list, so emotes retired before 2020 are found by
  frequency, not by the list). For Twitch channels, emotes are identified by frequency and capitalization and
  checked by hand.

## Predictions
1. "regarded" barely exists before 2020. The plain r-word is common in DGG chat in every year, and "regarded" as
   its softened stand-in is under 2% of the r-word's count in 2019 (it is a later house style).
2. Old DGG's top emotes are its own: at least 6 of DGG's top 10 emotes in every year are destiny.gg emotes
   (PEPE, YEE, OverRustle-type), not Twitch globals.
3. PEPE beats YEE in total uses across 2014-2020, but YEE wins at least one full year.
4. Destiny's Twitch chat is more emote-heavy and less wordy than destiny.gg chat: higher emote-only share and a
   shorter median message, with Twitch globals (Kappa, LUL, PogChamp) among its top emotes.
5. The heaviest chatters dominate DGG more than Twitch: the top 1% of DGG accounts write a larger share of messages
   than the top 1% of Destiny's Twitch chat accounts.
6. Overlap follows community lines. Of the adjacent channels, Hutch's chatters overlap most with DGG
   (as a share of Hutch's chatters). Asmongold, Ludwig, Moistcr1tikal and Badbunny overlap least.
7. The Nickjfuentes overlap with DGG is concentrated in a few months around Destiny-Fuentes interactions, and
   is near zero outside them.
8. DGG message volume rises over the archive and is highest in 2019-2020.
9. h3h3 chat in this archive predates the Hasan and Destiny feuds, so "pestiny", "fungi" and the other 2026
   snark markers are absent or near zero there.

## Scoring
Each prediction gets held, partly held or missed, misses included, in docs/orl-archive-results.md.
