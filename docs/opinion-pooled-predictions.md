# Pooled opinion sheet: predictions (written Nov 1, 2026, before anyone codes the sheet)

Sample: all comments with 8+ words in 13 political subs, pooled over the five weeks (Aug 18, Sep 8, Sep 22, Oct 13, Oct 20).
Each comment mentions one of four subjects: Trump (trump|maga), Hasan (hasan|hasanabi|piker), Israel (israel, israeli, idf,
netanyahu, bibi, zionis*), or Ukraine (ukraine, ukrainian, zelensky*, kyiv). A cell needs 60+ mentions to be sampled, and
45 cells qualify. Up to 30 comments are drawn per cell (seed 20261101) and shuffled, for 1,350 items. The coder sees the
subject and the text, never the sub, week or score. Codes are P/A/B/N, with Trump coded as in the earlier sheet: comments
about MAGA as a movement count toward Trump.

1. Trump: in neoliberal, thebulwark, pakman, Destiny, dsa, Hasan_Piker and samharris, at least 75% of the coded P/A comments
   are against him. Conservative is the only sub where pro comments are the majority. PCM is split, with pro between 30% and 60%.
2. Hasan: in Destiny, neoliberal, thebulwark and PCM, against is the majority of P/A comments. In Hasan_Piker, pro is the
   majority but stays under 80%.
3. Israel: against is over 80% of P/A in Palestine, TankieTheDeprogram, dsa and Hasan_Piker. Pro is the majority in
   Conservative and neoliberal. Destiny and IsraelPalestine fall between 35% and 65% pro.
4. Ukraine: pro is over 85% in neoliberal, thebulwark, ukraine and Destiny. TankieTheDeprogram is the only sub where against
   is the majority.
5. N (no lean) is the most common code in at least a third of the cells, so per-cell P/A counts are often under 15.
   Cells with fewer than 10 P/A comments are reported but not scored.

### Scoring
1. **Mostly held.** Every listed sub is 95-100% against Trump, and Conservative is the only sub where pro comments are the
   majority (73%). The miss is PCM: I predicted 30-60% pro and it came out at 12% (4-31). In this sample, Trump comments in PCM
   read like everyone else's.
2. **Mostly held.** Against is the majority in Destiny, neoliberal, PCM and thebulwark. Bulwark is only 56% against, with
   an interval that crosses 50%. Hasan_Piker came out 88% pro against my under-80% guess, but the interval (64-97)
   includes 80, so this miss is soft. Two results I didn't predict: pakman and dsa are split on Hasan (54% and 56% pro).
3. **Partly missed.** Palestine, Tankie, dsa and Hasan_Piker are all over 90% against Israel. Conservative is pro (68%). The
   misses: neoliberal is not pro-Israel (30%, but only 10 P/A, with 20 of 30 coded N), and Destiny is 67% pro, just outside my
   35-65 band. IsraelPalestine is 40%, inside the band. thebulwark is 84% against, which I didn't predict.
4. **Missed.** The four subs I named are 88-96% pro-Ukraine, as predicted. Tankie is not the only sub where against is the
   majority: Hasan_Piker (36% pro) and Palestine (31%) are too, and dsa is split (47%). Conservative is 64% pro, higher than
   I would have guessed.
5. **Missed.** N is the most common code in 9 of 45 cells, short of a third. Only one cell (IsraelPalestine x Ukraine) has
   fewer than 10 P/A comments.

### Caveats
- One coder. In the earlier Trump sheet, the second coder found that I lean toward "against" on comments about the
  administration. The Trump cells were extreme enough that this lean cannot change who wins any cell. It could move the
  Hasan, Israel and Ukraine middle cells by several points.
- The keyword sample picks up any mention. Many comments name the subject in passing, so "pro" and "against" here mean
  sentiment in comments that mention the subject. They are not a poll of each sub.
- A second coder on a 150-item subset would firm up the middle cells: Hasan in dsa, pakman and bulwark; Israel in Destiny,
  samharris, PCM and IsraelPalestine; Ukraine in dsa, Conservative and Hasan_Piker.
