# OverRustle archive (to Apr 30, 2020): results, first 12 channels

Scored Nov 2, 2026, against docs/orl-archive-predictions.md. Source tables: ~/socialscope_data/orl_agg (hashed nicks).
Channels in this round: Destinygg, Destiny (Twitch), Asmongold, Badbunny, Moistcr1tikal, Ludwig, H3h3productions,
Hutch, Melina, Mindwavestv, Denims, Nickjfuentes. Second batch (Hasan, Vaush, Rajj, Kelly_jean, Invadervie, Athene,
Pokimane, Trainwreck, Reckful, Trihex) is queued and not scored here.

## Data
- Destinygg: 110.5M messages, 2,314 days (Dec 2013 to Apr 2020). Destiny Twitch: 30.0M, 1,874 days (Feb 2015 on).
  Asmongold: 54.6M (Sep 2016 on). Other channels are short windows, mostly Mar-Apr 2020; H3 is effectively
  Feb 2017 to May 2018; Nickjfuentes May 2018 to Apr 2019.
- Overlap = share of a channel's regulars (3+ messages that month) who also had 3+ messages in the other channel
  the same month, pooled over shared months.
- Caveats: (1) DGG and Twitch are separate name spaces, so DGG-Twitch overlap counts same-spelled nicks and is an
  approximation; Twitch-Twitch overlap is exact. (2) Bots are not yet removed; dropping each year's three biggest
  accounts moves top-1% shares by 1-3 points. (3) Tokens that equal any chat nick were dropped, which removed
  "triggered" from Asmongold. (4) The emote list is today's destiny.gg list, so retired emotes are missed.
  (5) Asmongold's logs nearly vanish in Mar 2019 and Jan-Mar 2020 (OverRustle outages).

## Scores
1. **Held (sanity check).** "regarded" is 0.3% of the r-word every year, and most of that is ordinary "regarded as".
   The r-word itself runs 12-20 per 10k in DGG through 2019, then 3.4 in early 2020.
2. **Partly held.** DGG-original emotes (OverRustle, DaFeels, SWEATSTINY, DANKMEMES, WhoahDude, DuckerZ, PEPE...)
   hold 6-9 of the top 10 from 2014 to 2018, then 4 of 10 in 2019 and 2020, when PepeLaugh, LULW, OMEGALUL and
   FeelsWeirdMan take over.
3. **Missed (half).** PEPE beats YEE overall (3.1M to 1.5M) and in every year from 2015; YEE never wins a full year
   (2014's "win" is 92 to 6, before either emote existed in earnest).
4. **Mostly missed.** DGG is more emote-only than Destiny's Twitch chat in every year (20-28% vs 4-21%, same emote
   set for both), and median length is equal or shorter. Twitch globals do lead Twitch chat (Kappa, LUL, PogChamp).
   The emote set may undercount Twitch-only emotes.
5. **Missed.** Twitch is the more concentrated chat: top 1% write 49-65% of Destiny Twitch messages vs 42-44% in
   DGG (2015-19). Twitch has 4-6x as many accounts, many of them one-off visitors, which drives this.
6. **Missed.** Highest overlap with DGG: Denims 16.6%, Mindwave 15.9%, Destiny Twitch 8.9%, Melina 8.5%,
   Badbunny 5.5%. Hutch is 1.0%, Fuentes 1.2%, Asmongold 0.4%, H3 0.3%, Moist and Ludwig 0.1%.
   Hutch's spring-2020 audience was political (Biden 219, Trump 199, Bernie 176 per 10k) but separate from DGG.
7. **Partly held, link untested.** Fuentes regulars in DGG: 17 of 209 (8%) in May 2018, 0-4% in every other month.
   The known Destiny-Fuentes debate (Nov 2017) predates his logs, so the May 2018 spike is not yet tied to an event.
8. **Held.** DGG messages per month rise from 0.87M (2014) to 1.84M (2019) and 2.06M (Jan-Apr 2020). Monthly
   chatters stay at 6-9k, so the growth is more messages per chatter.
9. **Held.** "pestiny" and "fungi" are zero in 2017-18 H3 chat.
10. **Missed on the number, held relative to peers.** Asmongold electoral vocabulary is 15-23 per 10k (62 in 2016),
    the same band as Ludwig (23) and Moist (11); Destiny Twitch 42-202, Hutch 955.
11. **Held, declining.** Strict culture-war index 21 (2017-18), 17 (2019), 10 (2020), above Ludwig (3) and Moist (9);
    mostly "cuck", which falls from 20 to 3. China/Hong Kong terms jump in 2019 (Blizzard's Hearthstone ban).
12. **Partly held.** DEI zero; devs and Sony present as game talk; "woke" 1-2 per 10k, likely mostly "woke up".
13. **Missed.** 2-7% of Asmongold regulars also chat in Destiny's Twitch chat (Fuentes side under 5%, as predicted).
    Destiny's chat overlaps with Asmongold's 5-10x more than H3, Moist or Ludwig do; the rise over 2016-19 tracks
    Asmongold's chat size (r = 0.9).

## Other findings
- The 2019-20 Destiny orbit is visible as its own community: Denims, Mindwave and Melina name Destiny 139-207 times
  per 10k messages, overlap 8-17% with DGG and 23-51% with Destiny's Twitch chat, and use the DGG emote DuckerZ.
- Badbunny drifts away from Destiny: about 30% of its regulars were in Destiny's Twitch chat in late 2017, 4-6% by 2020.
- Destiny's Twitch chat shortens from a 4-word median (2015) to 2 (2017 on) as it grows from 1.9M to 9.0M messages a year.

## Hasan, Vaush and Destiny (scored Nov 3, 2026, against predictions 14-18)
Coverage: Hasanabi Aug 2018 to Apr 2020 (34.3M messages); Vaushvidya Mar-Apr 2019 (131k); Vaushgg (vaush.gg site chat) May 2019 to Apr 2020 (4.0M).
Caveat: the nick filter zeroed the token "vaush" in any chat where an account named vaush posted (DGG, Vaushgg), so
"vaush" mention rates are only usable for Destiny Twitch, Hasanabi and Vaushvidya.

14. **Missed (far higher).** 24% of Hasan's regulars were also Destiny Twitch regulars overall; 42-53% from Nov 2018
    to Apr 2019, falling to 6% by Apr 2020. From Destiny's side the share is steady: 26-36% of Destiny's Twitch
    regulars chatted in Hasan's chat every month from Jan 2019 to Apr 2020. The decline on Hasan's side is his chat
    growing (1.7k to 39k regulars) around a constant 2.4-7.4k shared accounts.
15. **Not testable as written.** Vaushgg is the chat on Vaush's own site (vaush.gg, separate accounts, like destiny.gg),
    not a Twitch channel. Vaushvidya (his Twitch, logged only Mar-Apr 2019): 27-37% of regulars also in Destiny
    Twitch, 6-14% in DGG. Vaushgg (May 2019 on) against Twitch chats matches same-spelled nicks only, so its 1.4-4%
    is a floor and the drop from Vaushvidya is mostly the change of platform, not an audience change. The
    like-for-like site-to-site figure is Vaushgg in DGG: 2-4% a month (also a floor).
16. **Missed.** Destiny mentions per 10k: Vaushvidya 86, Vaushgg 39-58; Hasanabi 144 (2018), 48 (2019), 1.3 (2020).
    The 2020 near-zero in Hasan's chat is sharp enough to suggest the name was blocked by chat filters; unverified.
17. **Missed.** Destiny Twitch 2019: "hasan" 57 per 10k, "vaush" 0.8.
18. **Missed.** Hasan electoral rate 214 (2018), 157 (2019), 398 (2020); Hutch spring 2020 was 955, Badbunny 2020 637.
- 4% of Vaushgg regulars matched a Hasan Twitch regular by name (cross-platform floor).
