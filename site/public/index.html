socialscope bot tools

From a student research project on bots and coordination in Reddit communities: score any Reddit account in your own browser, and see the bots we found that Bot Bouncer then banned.

Check a Reddit account

Type a username to score it with our bot detector (v7.1). Everything runs in your browser: your browser fetches the account's public posts and comments from Arctic Shift, an independent Reddit archive, and the score is worked out on this page. Nothing is sent to this site, saved or shared, and the name you type never goes into the page address.

How to read it

The rating runs from 1 to 10. Each step marks one of the detector's tested lines: 5 is the loose line (about 7 in 100 ordinary accounts reach it), 7 is the 2% line, and 9 is the strict line (about 2 in 100 ordinary accounts). The detector was trained on accounts that the volunteer project Bot Bouncer banned. A high rating is a lead for a closer look, not proof. Real people get flagged too. The time-zone and location clues come from posting hours and place subreddits. They're rough guesses and aren't part of the score. Arctic Shift can run a few days behind Reddit, and only the newest 1,000 posts and 1,000 comments are read.

Bots we found that Bot Bouncer then banned

Each account here was flagged by our detector, read by two reviewers, and reported to Bot Bouncer, which then banned it. The label is Bot Bouncer's, not ours: every entry links to its listing, and the status is rechecked every week. If Bot Bouncer reverses a listing, the account comes off this page at the next rebuild. Only posts from an account's bot period are shown; contact handles and other users' names inside them are masked.

What this is

Two public tools from socialscope, a student research project on bots, coordination and opinion spread in Reddit communities around streamers. The code and write-ups are on GitHub. The project's study of destiny.gg chat has its own site, DGG chat stats. Until November 19, 2026, both tools below were tabs on that site.

Privacy

Ordinary Reddit users stay anonymous in everything the project publishes: names are replaced by a salted one-way code. The one exception is the Bots tab: Reddit accounts that Bot Bouncer has banned as bots, after we reported them, are shown by name with their bot-period posts. They are never linked to chat accounts or to real people. The Lookup tab scores only the account you type, inside your own browser. We never see the name or the result, and we publish no lookup results.
