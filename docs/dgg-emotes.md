# destiny.gg emotes (Nov 3, 2026)

All 325 emotes on the official destiny.gg emote list (pulled Nov 2, 2026), with how often each appeared in destiny.gg site chat
(OverRustle archive, per 100k messages), Destiny's Twitch chat, and 2026 Reddit comments by r/Destiny regulars (per 10k comments).
Machine-readable: `data/dgg_emotes.csv`. Crossover emotes typed on Reddit also have rows in docs/glossary.md.

How to read it:
- The archive ends Apr 30, 2020. 185 emotes never reach 1 per 100k messages in it. Most were added after 2020; a few may be
  missing because an emote name matched a chatter's nick (fixed by the --keep-file rerun only for glossary terms).
- Sources: [DGG wiki A-L](https://wiki.destiny.gg/view/Current_Emotes_(A-L)), [DGG wiki M-Z](https://wiki.destiny.gg/view/Current_Emotes_(M-Z)).
- Reddit lift = r/Destiny regulars' rate divided by the rate across all 18 subs. Emotes are images in chat, so on Reddit they
  only count when someone types the name. Nine clear the bar of 0.5 per 10k and 3x lift: OMEGALUL, PEPE, YEE, AMAZIN, SOY,
  SCHIZO, GIGACHAD, SWEEPING, dggL.
- Meanings come from the DGG wiki's Current Emotes pages (A-L and M-Z, read Nov 3, 2026), condensed. 283 of the 325 emotes
  have a wiki entry; the other 42 are blank (newer emotes such as WAOW, NOTHINGBURGER, RAGEBAIT and SNARKMAN).
