# DGG chat on election night 2026, and chat vs r/Destiny (predictions)

Written Nov 5, 2026, 2:10 PM ET, before reading the Nov 3-5 live log for this purpose or pulling r/Destiny for those
days. Already seen: daily totals on the site (69k messages Nov 3 UTC, 194k Nov 4 UTC), the glossary's trending
terms (state names, orbiter drama terms), and a peak of 377 messages/minute on Nov 3 before polls closed.

Data: live DGG log Nov 3-5 (bots dropped); r/Destiny posts and comments Nov 3-5 (to be pulled).
Election night window: Nov 3, 7 PM ET to Nov 4, 3 AM ET.

## Election night
EN1. Chat's busiest minute of the three days falls inside the election-night window and tops 1,000 messages.
EN2. The emote-only share is higher on election night than in the daytime hours before it.
EN3. Alert emotes (ALARMA, DinkDonk) spike before or with mentions of a state, not after: in the 5 biggest state
     mention spikes, the alert spike comes first or in the same minute.
EN4. Celebration emotes (FlawlessVictory, LifeIsGreat, PoroPls, GIGACHAD, CINEMA) outnumber despair emotes
     (PepeHands, Sadge, DEPRESSTINY, PAINSTINY, NoTears) over the whole night.
EN5. For the races chat cared most about, chat's mention spike comes within 10 minutes of the race being called
     (call times from AP/major outlets).

## Chat vs r/Destiny, Nov 3-5
CR1. Fewer than half of chat's 30 top bursting terms (Nov 3-5, vs chat's own baseline) are also elevated in
     r/Destiny comments those days.
CR2. When a topic spikes in both, chat spikes first; the median lead is 2 hours or more.
CR3. The orbiter drama takes a larger share of chat than of r/Destiny (drama terms per 10k messages vs per 10k
