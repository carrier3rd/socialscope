What this is

A side study from a student research project on online communities around streamers. It looks at destiny.gg (DGG) chat two ways: a live log of today's chat, and the OverRustle archive of chat logs up to April 2020.

Live data

A read-only logger reads DGG's public chat feed. It never posts. The DGG moderators okayed it. Logging began on November 3, 2026, and the numbers here refresh once a day. Bot accounts are left out of the counts.

Archive data

OverRustle logged DGG and many Twitch chats until spring 2020. Coverage differs a lot by chat: some have years, some have weeks. The archive's own gaps (outages, late additions) are not filled in.

Privacy

No messages, usernames or user identifiers are published. Usernames are replaced by a salted one-way code as soon as a message is read, and only totals, shares and rates leave the research machine. Overlap between chats is counted only in aggregate; no one is followed from one chat to another.

Limits

  • Twitch-to-Twitch overlap matches the same accounts. Overlap with DGG or vaush.gg, which have their own accounts, matches same-spelled names only, so it undercounts.
  • Words that are also someone's chat name were dropped from word counts, which removed a few ordinary words in some chats.
  • Emotes are matched against today's destiny.gg emote list, so emotes retired before 2020 are not counted.
  • Shared chatters show who was present in both chats, not what they thought of either streamer.
