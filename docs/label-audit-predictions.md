# Bot Bouncer label audit: predictions (written Nov 1, 2026, 3:55 PM ET, before reading any card)

Question: how many of Bot Bouncer's bans on aged accounts (100+ items over a year or more) look like automated or
inauthentic accounts when read by hand, and how often do random aged accounts look that way?

Sample: 120 cards, shuffled, group hidden: all 60 aged banned accounts from the September and August tests, and 60
aged random controls from the same panels. Each card shows activity totals, top subs, recent-30-day activity,
8 random items from the newest 200 and 4 from the oldest 200 pulled. Codes: B = automated, spam, karma farm,
repurposed or otherwise inauthentic; H = ordinary person; U = can't tell. When unsure between B and H, code U.

Predictions:
1. Banned cards read B: 35-55%. H: 25-45%. The rest U.
2. Random cards read B: under 5%. H: over 80%.
3. Among banned cards coded B, the most common reason is a break in the history (topic, language or style change,
   or a burst after a long silence), not the content of any single item.
